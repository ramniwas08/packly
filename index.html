Questions

Does it work with my theme?

Yes. Packly adds its parts through a standard theme app embed, so it works on any Online Store 2.0 theme and on older themes too. Shopify asks you to switch an app's storefront part on yourself, so the app has a button that opens your theme editor with it already on.

Do I need to change my theme code?

No. There is nothing to paste. If the pack choices land in the wrong spot on an unusual theme, the app embed has a setting for where to place them.

How is stock handled?

A pack is split into its items at checkout. Each item comes off its own stock, so you never keep a separate stock number for a pack.

Can shoppers build their own pack?

Yes. Make a Build-your-own pack: the shopper chooses the size, then a colour for each slot. You set one price for the pack.

What happens if I uninstall?

Your products stay in your store. Shopify keeps Packly's settings for a limited time and restores them if you reinstall. Save a backup from the app's Set-up tab first to be safe.
