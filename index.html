Packly – Bundle & Pack Builder

A Shopify app for stores that sell packs of the same product in different colours and sizes.

Packly lets a shopper choose a single item, a ready-made pack, or build their own pack colour by colour and size by size. At checkout the pack is split into the items inside it, so each colour and size is picked, packed and taken off stock, while the pack keeps its own price.
